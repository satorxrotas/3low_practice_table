Rules on the board

How to play

  1. Pick up all 5 dice.
  2. Roll at the center target and let go before your hand crosses the foul line.
  3. Check the roll: instant wins, dice touching the −1 target, special combinations, single 3s.
  4. Keep at least 1 die per roll, building the lowest score. Pick up the rest and roll again.
  5. Say your total, then pass the dice.
  6. Lowest score each round wins 1 round point. 3 round points wins the game.

How this table reads them

  • Every die scores its face, except 3s, which score 0.
  • Combos are scored on the dice you keep, even when they came from different throws. Combo dice score the combo's value instead of their faces: three 6s score 0, a full house scores −1, 4 of a kind scores −2.
  • Five of a kind and a large straight are instant wins, whether they land in one throw or you build them across your turn.
  • A die touching the target is worth −1 when you keep it. If its face would complete a better combo (a full house, 4 of a kind, large straight or five of a kind), the table automatically counts it that way instead.
  • The first bad throw of your turn carries no penalty.
  • A die that rolled off can't be part of a combo.
  • A tie for lowest means a redo: the round starts over for the same point.
  • The winner of the last round starts the next one, and play goes clockwise from there. After a tie, the redo starts with the same player.
  • Kept dice go in your HOME section. A kept target die goes in GOOD; a kept die that rolled off goes in BAD, along with a +1 marker for each foul.
